Home Warranty Customers: What to Expect

GET Heating & Air is an independent contractor. Your home warranty company sent us to your home. We don't work for them, and we don't decide what your plan covers.

  1. You file a claim with your warranty company. They send the job to us.
  2. We call you to schedule. Appointment times depend on our schedule and your warranty company's rules.
  3. You pay your service fee when we arrive. Your warranty company sets this fee, not us. You owe it whatever we find and whether or not the repair is approved.
  4. We find the problem and report it. We tell your warranty company what's wrong, what caused it and what it takes to fix.
  5. Your warranty company decides. They approve or deny the repair, choose which parts they'll pay for and set the timeline. This can take hours or days.
  6. We do the approved work. If something isn't covered, we'll tell you what it is and give you a price. You decide whether to pay for it yourself.
Our technicians can't approve, deny or change a claim. If you disagree with a decision, call your warranty company. Arguing with our technician won't change the outcome.

What Your Warranty Usually Won't Pay For

Every plan is different, so check your contract. These are the items warranty companies most often refuse to pay for:

  • Service and diagnostic fees. You owe your service fee even if the repair is denied.
  • Code violations and permits. Fixing existing code problems and pulling permits are usually your cost. Some top-tier plans cover a small amount.
  • Structural changes. Cutting into walls, ceilings or floors, or changing closets and platforms to fit new equipment.
  • Lack of maintenance. Damage caused by dirty filters, dirty coils or skipped yearly service. Routine maintenance is never covered.
  • Cosmetic damage like dents and scratches that don't affect how the system runs.
  • Problems that started before your coverage did.
  • Ductwork, vents and registers. Often not covered, or only partly.
  • Refrigerant over your plan's limit.
  • Cranes or special equipment, for example to lift a unit onto a roof.
  • Custom sheet metal made to fit new equipment.
  • Haul-away and disposal fees for old equipment and refrigerant.
  • Damage from outside causes: power surges, storms, pests, flooding or bad past repairs.
  • Window and portable units, fireplaces and fuel tanks.
  • Add-ons: UV lights, air purifiers, humidifiers, dehumidifiers and similar items.

When something isn't covered, we'll tell you exactly what it is and what it costs to do it anyway. You don't have to accept.

American Home Shield Customers

These are American Home Shield's published limits for heating and air. Your plan level decides what you get. Your contract is final, so check it if anything here doesn't match.

Dollar limits

  • $5,000 per unit for standard central AC, heat pumps, furnaces and package units. Anything over that is yours.
  • $1,500 per unit for geothermal, water-source heat pumps, and glycol, hot-water or steam systems.
  • Up to $1,000 if we have to cut through concrete to reach lines or parts.

Refrigerant

  • ShieldSilver and ShieldGold: AHS pays $10 per pound. You pay us the rest of the refrigerant cost at the visit.
  • ShieldPlatinum: refrigerant is covered with no limit.

Code violations, permits and modifications

  • ShieldPlatinum: up to $250 total.
  • All other plans: not covered. These costs are yours.

Usually covered

Breakdowns from normal wear and tear, rust, corrosion or age, including compressors, coils, condenser fans, furnaces, heat strips, burners, blower motors, thermostats, condensate drain lines and hardwired parts. If a replacement has to meet new federal efficiency or refrigerant rules, AHS covers the needed upgrades up to your plan limit.

Not covered

Window and portable units, fireplaces, fuel tanks, humidifiers, dehumidifiers, UV lights and air purifiers, routine maintenance, cosmetic damage, cranes, custom sheet metal, and disposal of old equipment or refrigerant.

If AHS doesn't cover something, the balance is between you and GET Heating & Air. We'll give you the price before we do the work. AHS sets the rules; we don't.

Common Questions

Why do I have to pay a service fee if my repair was denied?

The fee is part of your warranty contract and pays for the visit and diagnosis. Your warranty company requires us to collect it.

Why do I owe money for refrigerant?

Many plans only pay part of the refrigerant cost. With AHS ShieldSilver and ShieldGold, for example, AHS pays $10 per pound and you pay the rest.

Why is my repair taking so long?

Your warranty company has to approve the repair and often has to approve or order the parts too. We start as soon as they give the go-ahead.

Can I ask for a different part or a new system?

Your warranty company decides that. We can give you a price for anything beyond what they approve.

My claim was denied. What now?

Call your warranty company to dispute it. You can also pay us directly to do the repair.

Does the online form book my appointment?

No. It sends us a request. You are not on the schedule until we call or text you to confirm a day and time.

Who do I call about my appointment?

Request service online or call us at (501) 812-9252 about scheduling. Call your warranty company about coverage, approvals and claim status.
