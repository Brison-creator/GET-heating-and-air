Common Questions

How much does a repair cost?

It depends on what's broken. We diagnose it first and give you the price up front before we do anything.

Should I repair or replace my system?

If your system is over 10–12 years old, breaks down often or your bills keep climbing, replacing it may save money. We'll give you a free estimate and an honest opinion either way.

How long does a new system install take?

Most standard installs are done in a day. Bigger jobs, like new ductwork, can take longer.

How often should my system be serviced?

At least once a year, ideally twice: once before summer and once before winter. A tune-up covers filters, coils, refrigerant, electrical connections and safety checks.

Do you work on my brand?

Yes. We service all major brands.
