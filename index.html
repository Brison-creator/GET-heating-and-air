Home Warranty Customers: What to Expect

GET Heating & Air is an independent contractor. Your home warranty company sent us to your home. We don't work for them, and we don't decide what your plan covers.

  1. You file a claim with your warranty company. They send the job to us.
  2. We call you to schedule. Appointment times depend on our schedule and your warranty company's rules.
  3. You pay your service fee when we arrive. Your warranty company sets this fee, not us. You owe it whatever we find and whether or not the repair is approved.
  4. We find the problem and report it. We tell your warranty company what's wrong, what caused it and what it takes to fix.
  5. Your warranty company decides. They approve or deny the repair, choose which parts they'll pay for and set the timeline. This can take hours or days.
  6. We do the approved work. If something isn't covered, we'll tell you what it is and give you a price. You decide whether to pay for it yourself.
Our technicians can't approve, deny or change a claim. If you disagree with a decision, call your warranty company. Arguing with our technician won't change the outcome.

What Your Warranty Usually Won't Pay For

Every plan is different, so check your contract. These are the items warranty companies most often refuse to pay for:

  • Service and diagnostic fees. You owe your service fee even if the repair is denied.
  • Code upgrades. If current building code requires changes to make the repair, such as electrical, venting, drain lines or refrigerant type, those costs are usually yours.
  • Structural changes. Cutting into walls, ceilings or floors, or changing closets and platforms to fit new equipment.
  • Lack of maintenance. Damage caused by dirty filters, clogged drain lines, dirty coils or skipped yearly service.
  • Problems that started before your coverage did.
  • Ductwork, vents and registers. Often not covered, or only partly.
  • Refrigerant over your plan's limit.
  • Cranes or special equipment, for example to reach rooftop units.
  • Permits, haul-away and disposal fees.
  • Damage from outside causes: power surges, storms, pests, flooding or bad past repairs.
  • Add-ons: thermostats, air purifiers, humidifiers and similar items.

When something isn't covered, we'll tell you exactly what it is and what it costs to do it anyway. You don't have to accept.

Common Questions

Why do I have to pay a service fee if my repair was denied?

The fee is part of your warranty contract and pays for the visit and diagnosis. Your warranty company requires us to collect it.

Why is my repair taking so long?

Your warranty company has to approve the repair and often has to approve or order the parts too. We start as soon as they give the go-ahead.

Can I ask for a different part or a new system?

Your warranty company decides that. We can give you a price for anything beyond what they approve.

My claim was denied. What now?

Call your warranty company to dispute it. You can also pay us directly to do the repair.

Who do I call about my appointment?

Call us at (501) 812-9252 about scheduling. Call your warranty company about coverage, approvals and claim status.
